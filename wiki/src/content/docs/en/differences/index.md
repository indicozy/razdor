What Razdor adds to *Discord Times*: new features, experimental scenario rules, completion marks, and sound.

- [Overview of features](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/overview/): every addition, by theme.
- [Completion marks](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/run-marks/): colored checkmarks on the scenario list and their reasons.
- [Experimental scenario rules](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/experimental-rules/): six switches for a new game.
- [Default rules](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/default-rules/): where Razdor's rules without the switches differ from the original.
- [Sound and music](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/sound-music/): what can be set, and how Razdor takes the sound from the game.
- [Lighting, mist and water](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/map-look/): three new map look settings, and 2.5D buildings that stand on their ground.
