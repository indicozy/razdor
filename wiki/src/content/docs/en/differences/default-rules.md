Razdor plays by the original's rules. Below is everything that differs **by default**, without the [experimental rules](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/experimental-rules/). Five differences that used to be here are now experimental switches: by default they follow the original's rule (see below).

## Deliberate differences

Five former differences (bless once a turn, poison and the Undead, the spell book, the merchant discount from an item, Rear Service) are now experimental switches: by default the original's rule applies. You can turn them on in the "..." window on the scenario screen, see [experimental rules](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/experimental-rules/).

* **Wide front row:** your units step only to the 12 cells the screen shows.
* **Formation after a battle:** units go back to their cells (the "Keep formation" option, which can be turned off).
* **The fallen keep their worn items.** You can take them off the corpse and put them in the pack.
* **The Caster discount** works when the hero's army has a Caster. In the original it depended on the last army recounted, often someone else's.
* **Market lists** are sorted cheapest first.
* **An event chain** stops at 32 levels deep, so the game cannot hang.

## Original bugs fixed

Razdor fixes bugs that made the original behave other than intended. For example:

* a win where the enemy had only surrendering units left counted as a defeat;
* Life's Eternal Gift lowered defence instead of raising it;
* ghosts with high magic power could not cast;
* the hero's Rear Service cut the AI armies' wages;
* a mana shortage left the elementals unpaid for good;
* the buildings' mana stock overflowed to zero;
* resurrection checked its price in the wrong currency;
* landing from a ship and stepping ashore tested the wrong cell.

## Known differences still being fixed

* **AI ships** sail about twice as fast as in the original.

## Razdor's additions

The deployment phase before a battle, quick battle, battle restart, cheats, quick and pinned saves, the «Максимум» AI. All are described in the [feature overview](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/overview/).
