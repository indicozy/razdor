## First steps

<Steps>

1. **You need your own copy of Discord Times.** Razdor does not include the game: it reads its files. How to prepare it: [Installing the game](/razdor/wiki/en/start/install-game/).

2. **Download Razdor and run it** next to the game: [Installing Razdor](/razdor/wiki/en/start/install-razdor/). On a phone: [Android](/razdor/wiki/en/phones/android/) or [iOS](/razdor/wiki/en/phones/ios/).

3. **Learn the screen and the controls:** [Interface](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/interface/) and [keys and gamepad](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/controls/).

4. **Play.** What Razdor adds to the original: [Feature overview](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/overview/).

</Steps>
