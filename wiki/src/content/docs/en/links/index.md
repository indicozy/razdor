This wiki is about Razdor. Other sites cover the game itself, its units, items and maps in depth (most of them in Russian).

## Razdor

- [Razdor's site](https://indicozy.github.io/razdor/en.html): download, installation, what's new.
- [Razdor on GitHub](https://github.com/indicozy/razdor): source code, releases, bug reports.
- [Razdor video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p7g0yvwtEeM).

## Game wikis and references

- [Discord Times wiki on Fandom](https://discorttimes.fandom.com/ru/wiki/) (Russian): units, heroes, scenarios.
- [Ludovik's Pantry](https://dt-items.github.io/) (Russian): every item in the game, an item editor for mods, comparisons and a tier list.
- [Discord Times on Igrowiki](https://igrowiki.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%92%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B0) (Russian): the game and unit upgrades.

## Guides

- [A detailed guide to the map editor](https://www.playground.ru/thorny_times/guide/vremena_razdora_podrobnoe_rukovodstvo_po_sozdaniyu_kart_i_rabote_v_redaktore-1853922) (Playground, Russian): making maps, the original editor's bugs and limits.
