From version 1.3.0 Razdor's code is released under **GPL-3.0** (version 3 only) with two additional terms. Versions up to and including 1.2.4 came out under MIT and stay under MIT.

## For players

Nothing changes for players: Razdor is free, you may download it, install it and share it. Community maps and mods are not affected. The files of Discord Times itself are not part of Razdor: it reads them from your own install.

## For forks and builds

You may change the code, build your own versions and publish them. The terms:

* **Open code.** If you publish your build, its source code must be available under the same GPL-3.0.
* **Credit.** The authors screen (add one reachable from the main menu if there is none) and the README must keep the line:
  `Based on Razdor by indicozy — https://github.com/indicozy/razdor`
* **Name.** Your version may not be called "Razdor" or «Раздор». Saying that it is based on Razdor is allowed, and required.

Full text: the [LICENSE](https://github.com/indicozy/razdor/blob/main/LICENSE) and [NOTICE](https://github.com/indicozy/razdor/blob/main/NOTICE) files.

## For contributors

For your code to be accepted into Razdor, you sign the contributor agreement ([CLA](https://github.com/indicozy/razdor/blob/main/CLA.md)) once: a bot asks for it on your first pull request. You keep the rights to your code; the agreement lets Razdor use your contribution.
