The **"Sound, graphics and gameplay settings"** window opens with the **"Sound settings"** button on the bottom bar or from the main menu. A column of tabs runs down its left side; the right side shows the settings of the selected tab.

The **"General"** tab is the original's: music and sound volumes and the checkboxes. On a phone, the touchpad mode checkbox comes after them.

The other tabs are Razdor's:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Map** | graphics quality (first row: "Original", "Low", "Medium", "High"; one row sets the map resolution, light, shadows, fog, water, weather, and animation; "Custom", if something has been changed manually; [details](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/performance/)); map scale and locking; map scrolling speed; map view (flat, 2.5D, third-person, or first-person); in 2.5D and first-person views, figure size and rotation with two fingers; in first-person views, "Distance", "Bend Start", "Bend Strength", "Camera Height", "Camera Tilt", "Field of View", and "Bend and Camera", and in third-person, "Camera follows hero" ([details](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/first-person/)); lighting (steady brightness, on by default, shadows, light quality), fog instead of blackness, dynamic water, weather, and dynamic map ([details](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/map-look/)); map animation ("On", "Only nearby", "Off", [details](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/performance/)); depth model for mods in 2.5D; map resolution and FPS limit; map continuation beyond the edge; battles on the map; army list on the minimap; leader's name; minimap angle and reset |
| **Combat and Army** | hero movement speed; combat animation speed; "Impact Shake" (the battlefield shakes upon impact, view only; disabled by default); AI strength in combat; unit participation in combat; saving formation after battle; mage protection; army formation; experience upon leveling up |
| **Screen** | screen (window or full); interface scale; bottom panel size (phone, only in landscape); font; text size; large message windows; screen orientation (Android); language |
| **Controls** | hotkeys (button **"Keys..."**) |
| **Other** | updates and **"Check"** button; game update from archive; "After Map Summary" ([Chronicle](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/chronicle/)); "Battery Saving" (when nothing is happening on the map, frames are drawn at 10 per second; disabled by default); "Performance Test" (button **"Run"**: measures how fast the game runs on this device, [details](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/performance/)); "Debug mode" (disabled by default; writes a performance log and shows a bar on every screen: "Debug mode enabled — FPS may drop"; active immediately); "Send the log…" (game log, play log, and last performance report in one file for error reporting; on Android, the file is saved via the system dialog, on a computer, the log folder is opened); Android only: "Move saves from the old Razdor folder…" (saves from the old version) |

Short explanations stand under their rows. Long ones sit behind a **"?"** button at the end of the row: click it (on a computer, hovering is enough) and the explanation appears. A click elsewhere or Esc closes it.

You can switch tabs at any time; changes on one tab are kept when you move to another. **"Apply"** keeps everything that was changed on all tabs; **"Cancel"** (or the close box, or Esc) puts the settings back as they were when the window opened.

:::tip
The F1 key and the **"Keys..."** button open the same key list as the **"Controls"** tab.
:::
