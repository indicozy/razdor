The **"Sound, graphics and gameplay settings"** window opens with the **"Sound settings"** button on the bottom bar or from the main menu. A column of tabs runs down its left side; the right side shows the settings of the selected tab.

The **"General"** tab is the original's: music and sound volumes and the checkboxes. On a phone, the touchpad mode checkbox comes after them.

The other tabs are Razdor's:

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Map** | the map's zoom and its lock; the map's scroll speed; the map view (flat, 2.5D, third person or first person); with 2.5D and the two views from the hero, the sprite size and the two-finger turn; in the views from the hero, "Far ground", "Curve start", "Curve strength", "Camera height", "Camera tilt", "Field of view" and "Curve and camera", and in third person "Camera follows the hero" ([more](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/first-person/)); lighting (shadows, light quality), mist instead of black, moving water, weather and the living map ([more](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/map-look/)); the map animation ("On", "Near only", "Off", [more](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/performance/)); the depth model for mods in 2.5D; the map resolution and the frame rate cap; extending the map past its edges; battles on the map; the army list beside the minimap; the leader's name; the minimap's corner and its reset |
| **Battle and army** | the hero's walking speed; the battle animation speed; "Hit shake" (the battle field shakes on a hit, view only; off by default); battle AI strength; unit battle participation; keeping the formation after battle; max guards casters; army arrangement; experience on promotion |
| **Screen** | the screen (windowed or full); the interface scale; the bottom bar size (phone, held sideways only); the font; the text size; large message windows; screen orientation (Android); the language |
| **Controls** | keyboard shortcuts (the **"Keys..."** button) |
| **Other** | updates and the **"Check now"** button; updating the game from an archive; "Summary after a map" ([Chronicle](/razdor/wiki/en/differences/chronicle/)); "Battery saver" (when nothing moves on the map, frames are drawn 10 a second; off by default) |

Short explanations stand under their rows. Long ones sit behind a **"?"** button at the end of the row: click it (on a computer, hovering is enough) and the explanation appears. A click elsewhere or Esc closes it.

You can switch tabs at any time; changes on one tab are kept when you move to another. **"Apply"** keeps everything that was changed on all tabs; **"Cancel"** (or the close box, or Esc) puts the settings back as they were when the window opened.

:::tip
The F1 key and the **"Keys..."** button open the same key list as the **"Controls"** tab.
:::
