The **"Sound, graphics and gameplay settings"** window opens with the **"Sound settings"** button on the bottom bar or from the main menu. Its top part is the original's: music and sound volumes, the map's scroll speed and the hero's walking speed, the battle animation speed, and the checkboxes.

Under them are five Razdor group buttons. Each opens its own window with only that group's settings; the **"Ok"** button goes back to the settings window.

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Map...** | the map's zoom and its lock; the map view (flat or 2.5D); with 2.5D, the sprite size and the two-finger turn; extending the map past its edges; battles on the map; the army list beside the minimap; the leader's name; the minimap's corner and its reset |
| **Battle and army...** | battle AI strength; unit battle participation; keeping the formation after battle; max guards casters; army arrangement; experience on promotion |
| **Screen...** | the screen (windowed or full); the interface scale; the bottom bar size (phone); the font; the text size; large message windows; screen orientation (Android); the language |
| **Controls...** | keyboard shortcuts (the **"Keys..."** button); touchpad mode (on a phone) |
| **Other...** | updates and the **"Check now"** button; updating the game from an archive |

Short explanations stand under their rows. Long ones sit behind a **"?"** button at the end of the row: click it (on a computer, hovering is enough) and the explanation appears. A click elsewhere or Esc closes it.

Changes made in a group window are kept. **"Apply"** keeps everything that was changed; **"Cancel"** in the settings window undoes only what was changed in that window.

:::tip
The F1 key and the **"Keys..."** button open the same key list as the **"Controls..."** group.
:::
