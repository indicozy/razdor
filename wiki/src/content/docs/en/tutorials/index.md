- [Game recordings](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/replays/): the recording, the watching screen, the timeline, battles and a recording that drifts.
- [Quick battle and Custom battle](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/quick-battle/): auto-battle, your own battles and the battle code.
- [Gear filter](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/gear/): searching items in the backpack and at the market.
- [How to translate a map](/razdor/wiki/en/tutorials/translate-map/): the map translator and the game data, step by step, checking it in Razdor and sharing a translation.
