# Mobile UI specs, 2026-10-30

From the first full `mobile-ui` run (72 cases: 18 phone screens × small 1080×2400 at 2.75 px/dp
and big 1440×3120 at 3.5 × portrait and landscape; the phone defaults: text size 1.25, large
message windows on). Limits: text under 12 sp too small, under 14 small; a touch target's shorter
side under 40 dp too small, under 48 small; two targets under 8 dp apart crowded. Sizes are the
small phone's in portrait unless said; the big phone's are within a few percent (both are about
393-411 dp wide). Game texts are left out on purpose: elements are named by their role and their
call site.

Status: **fixed** in this change, or **open** (needs the owner's design call; listed in
`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## S1. Touch targets under 48 dp (fixed: finger-sized hit areas)

Every target below goes through `widgets::button_state`. Its drawing is right; only its hit area
is too small for a finger.

| target | call site | hit area now (dp) |
|---|---|---|
| hire pill under a recruit card (barracks) | `building_view.rs` (hire pill, `chrome::pill_button`) | 53 × 11 |
| buy / sell button (store, market) | `building_view.rs` (`button_sounding`) | 49 × 18 |
| quick battle / watch / speed buttons | `battle_view.rs` (`button`) | 46-228 × 16-20 |
| settings and dialog buttons (OK, Cancel, Accept, More) | `main_menu.rs` (`settings_button`) | 49-69 × 15-18 |
| new game buttons (Next, Cancel, …, Start, Add a map) | `new_game.rs` (`Win::button`, hero buttons, `add_map_button`) | 22-181 × 17-21 |
| favourite star on a scenario row | `new_game.rs` (`star_r`) | 12 × 12 |
| save book rows | `saves.rs` (row hit) | 365 × 18 |
| spellbook filter button | `spellbook.rs` (`bottom_row`) | 86 × 10 |
| item group buttons and the search button | `item_filter.rs` | 17 × 13, 33 × 16 |
| title screen links and chips | `main_menu.rs` (`link`, the chip row) | 21-44 × 13, 30-35 × 48 |
| window close cross at the screen's top (landscape) | `chrome.rs` (`title_bar_styled` → `touch_hit`) | 44 × 19-26 |
| tabs of a building window | `building_view.rs` (tab row) | 156 × 43 |

**Fix.** On a phone (`display::dp_px()` is Some) `button_state` takes a *finger* hit area
(`widgets::finger_hit`) instead of the drawn rectangle: each side grown to 48 dp where there is
room, the drawing unchanged. Room is limited by:

- its window (the smallest window drawn in the last frame that holds the target), then the screen;
- the bottom bar's band (a target on the bar stays on the bar, one above it stays above);
- every other target or pointer area of the last frame (`button_state`, `tapped_in`, `mouse_in`
  rectangles) that does not contain this one: the growth stops at the midline between the two, so
  two neighbours' areas never overlap and a control with its own hit test (the unit grids, check
  boxes, sliders) never loses a tap to a grown neighbour;
- a growth blocked on one side goes to the other side, up to 48 dp in all.

The desktop keeps the drawn rectangles (the original's). A phone choice of Razdor, not parity.
The touch probe records the finger area, so the check measures what a finger gets.

## S2. The bottom bar's buttons (open)

The bar's eight oval buttons are 31 × 17 dp in portrait (`game_bar.rs`, `oval`) and its labels
7 sp. The finger area cannot grow them enough: the bar's band is about 33 dp tall in portrait and
the ovals sit side by side. Needs a phone layout of the bar (taller in portrait, or fewer, larger
buttons with the rest in a menu): the owner's design call.

## S3. Text under 12 sp nearly everywhere outside messages (open)

«Размер текста» scales message, pop-up and hint text only (`display::text_for`). The rest (window
titles, labels, stats, list rows, the bar's numbers) is the original's 800×600 layout scaled to the
screen's width: in portrait about 1.35 px per original px, 5-7 sp. Raising it needs phone layouts
of the windows (wrap, fewer columns, scroll) or a phone-wide text base for labels: the owner's
design call. The check's numbers per screen are in the run's report.

## S4. Controls the probe does not see (open, low risk now)

Seen in the marked shots with no outline: the unit grids' cards (army, battle, building), check
boxes and sliders in the settings, scrollbar arrows, the scenario list's rows, the key list. They
test the pointer themselves (`mouse_in` + `clicked`). The cards are large enough; check boxes,
slider arrows and scrollbar arrows look about 15-20 dp. Next step: route each through
`button_state` (then S1's finger area applies and the probe measures them). Since S1 treats their
`mouse_in` areas as obstacles, a grown neighbour cannot take their taps meanwhile.

## S5. Crowded pairs (mostly fixed by S1)

Pairs closer than 8 dp: the bar's ovals (S2), the tabs and the exit button of a building window,
the settings' Accept and Cancel, the new game window's bottom row, the title screen's chips. With S1
their finger areas meet at the midline instead of overlapping; the drawings stay as close as they
were.
