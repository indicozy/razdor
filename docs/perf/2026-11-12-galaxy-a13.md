# Galaxy A13 (Samsung Remote Test Lab), 2026-11-12

The second lab phone: Galaxy A13 with a Mali-G52 MC2 (the Helio G80 model, SM-A137F: 2× Cortex-A75 + 6× A55),
1080×2273, Razdor 1.4.1 (main at ecabad0e) **running the 32-bit (armeabi-v7a) build** (`app_process32`,
"android arm" in the log), «Все пресеты» on «Другой берег», with the `profile` file on (frame probe).

## Reading

- Near 2.5D 38.5 FPS (A04: 24.5), whole map 12.1 (A04: 8.3), flat 37.2 / 14.8.
- **The "objects" stage (the map's sprites) is the CPU cost again:** 12–13 ms of 16 ms CPU near, 67–79 ms
  of 71–84 ms CPU on the whole map. Ground under 0.5 ms, fog 0, the rest 3.5–5 ms.
- **«Среднее»/«Высокое» near: the GPU** (present 40–51 ms against 9.5 ms for «Оригинал»), 14 FPS.
- «Низкое» again no better than «Оригинал» (33.0 vs 38.5 near).
- RSS 367–407 MB; first map load 2.5 s.
- The phone ran the 32-bit library: a 64-bit capable SoC on a 32-bit userland, or the APK's ABI choice;
  worth checking, as the arm64 build may be faster.
