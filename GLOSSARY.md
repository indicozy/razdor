# Razdor

Razdor plays Discord Times' scenarios by the original rules; this glossary names the concepts of
its views and art that are Razdor's own.

## Views

**Top view**:
The world map seen from DT's own high three-quarter camera, the way the original draws it.
_Avoid_: map view, iso view

**2.5D view**:
The top view with each object sprite split into depth cards that stand at their own ground points.
_Avoid_: depth view, 3D view

**First-person view**:
The world map seen from the hero's eyes at ground level, walking DT's own cell moves.
_Avoid_: FPV, 3D mode, eye view

**Yaw step**:
One of the 8 directions (45° apart) the camera can look at an object from.
_Avoid_: angle, facing (a facing belongs to a figure, not the camera)

## Art

**Rotated view**:
A pre-generated picture of one object sprite seen from one yaw step at one camera height (top or eye level).
_Avoid_: rotation, angle sprite, turnaround

**View set**:
All rotated views of one sprite, keyed by the sprite's pixel hash.
_Avoid_: rotation sheet, view pack
